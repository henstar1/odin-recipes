Focaccia Bread

Description

Ingredients

  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 2 tablespoons bread flour
  • ¼ teaspoon active dry yeast
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 4 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil, or more as needed
  • 1 ⅔ cups water, at room temperature
  • 1 tablespoon minced fresh rosemary
  • 1 tablespoon flaky sea salt

Steps

  1. Add bread flour and yeast to a large bowl and stir together
  2. Add kosher salt, oil, and water and stir
  3. Add extra oil or water if needed to create wet sticky dough
  4. Cover bowl and leave in room temperature for 12-14 hours
  5. Transfer to oiled pan for baking (preferably around 16 x 12 inches)
  6. Press dough into shape of pan
  7. Fold dough into thirds, once horizontally and then vertically
  8. Cover and let sit for 1 hour
  9. Press dough flat and repeat step 7 and step 8
  10. Press dough once more, drizzle with olive oil, cover and set for 2 hours
  11. Preheat oven to 425 degrees fahrenheit
  12. Add rosemary and flaky sea salt over the dough and bake for 30 minutes or until browned
  13. Let cool on wire rack and then eat
