Garbanzo Salad

Description

Ingredients

  • 3 cans of chickpea/garbanzo beans
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1/2 jar of olives
  • 3/4 block of feta cheese
  • Jar to make dressing
  • 1 lemon
  • Red pepper flakes
  • Olive Oil
  • White wine vinegar
  • Parsley

Steps

  1. Drain and rince garbanzo beans and add to large bowl
  2. Cut up vegetables to desired size and add to bowl
  3. Crumble feta and add to bowl
  4. Get the jar to make the dressing
  5. Add olive oil, a little vinegar, red pepper flakes, lemon juice (squeeze using your prefered method), and chopped parsley (after rinsing)
  6. Shake up dressing to emulsify and add to bowl
  7. Toss all the ingredients and it's ready to eat
