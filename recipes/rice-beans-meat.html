Rice, Beans, Meat

Description

Rice, beans, and ground beef. It doesn't get any simpler than this. Grab a pan and something to cook rice and get started with this recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet of rice (microwaveable or otherwise)
  • 1 can of beans (kidney beans are prefered)
  • 1/2 onion (optional)
  • 1 seasoning packet (optional)

Steps

  1. Dice the onion if desired
  2. Oil a pan over medium-high heat and put the onion in to caramelize
  3. Once mostly caramelized, add ground beef to pan
  4. Separate ground beef into smaller parts using a spoon or other utensil to ensure everything gets cooked fully
  5. Add in seasoning packet if desired and continue to monitor ground beef
  6. Once ground beef is completely cooked, drain can of beans and add just the beans to the pan
  7. Lower heat to low-medium to keep beans and meat warm
  8. Microwave or otherwise cook rice (start this step as early as desired to everything is ready at the same time)
  9. Add rice to a large bowl and beans and meat on top
  10. Eat with a fork or chips
